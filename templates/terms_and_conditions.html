Terms and conditions

These terms describe the intended use and limits of the current Rythu Mitra prototype. They must be reviewed by the project operator before public launch.

Last updated: 15 October 2026. Version: pre-launch draft.

Pre-launch requirement: the operator's identity and contact details must be added, the final hosting and service providers must be confirmed, and these terms must be reviewed for the intended deployment and applicable law.

1. What Rythu Mitra is

Rythu Mitra is a farmer decision-support project intended to organize farm records and provide access to crop-planning tools, preliminary crop-health analysis, weather information when configured, reported mandi prices, calculation tools, and agriculture-related records. The application is not itself a government agency, agricultural university, insurance company, bank, seed certifier, or market operator.

2. Who may use it

You must provide accurate information for your own account, keep your password confidential, and use only the account and permissions assigned to you. Do not impersonate another person, access another user's records without permission, interfere with the service, or use the application to upload unlawful, harmful, or unauthorized material.

3. Agricultural and model information

Crop recommendations, crop-health results, risk notes, and any generated guidance are preliminary decision-support outputs. They may be incomplete, incorrect, outdated, or unsuitable for a particular farm. Image quality, model limitations, missing inputs, local conditions, and provider data quality can affect the output.

Confirm important decisions with a qualified local agricultural expert, soil test, product label, or relevant official source. Do not use a model result alone to diagnose a disease, choose a chemical treatment, or determine a safe dosage. Rythu Mitra does not guarantee yield, crop health, eligibility, revenue, market price, or profit.

4. Weather and market prices

Weather and market information is shown only when a configured provider or permitted dataset returns a usable result. Mandi prices are dated wholesale market observations, not a guarantee of the price you will receive at the farm gate. Transport, quality, commissions, timing, market access, and other costs may change the final amount.

Cached, stale, curated, user-entered, or model-generated data must be treated according to its displayed status. If a source is unavailable, the application may show no result.

5. Records, uploads and sharing

You are responsible for having permission to upload photos, documents, and personal information. Do not upload someone else's private documents or images without authorization. Use helper-sharing, listing, expert-question and community features only with the permissions and visibility rules shown in the application.

Submitting a damage report, complaint, insurance record, scheme record or grievance inside this application does not mean it has been filed with a government department, insurer, dealer, or other external organization. You must complete the relevant official process separately unless the interface explicitly confirms an integration with that organization.

6. Financial tools

Income, cost, storage, and market-comparison tools use the inputs and assumptions displayed to you. Results are estimates for comparison and record keeping, not financial advice, a quote, a guarantee, or a promise of future prices.

7. Availability and account security

The prototype may be changed, interrupted, or unavailable during development. Some functions require external credentials, compatible model files, or provider availability. Keep your sign-in details private and report suspected unauthorized access to the published project contact. Do not rely on this prototype as the only copy of important farm or financial records.

8. Intellectual property and feedback

Do not copy or redistribute third-party material unless you have the necessary rights. You may provide feedback about defects and usability. The operator must document any separate license that applies to the project's source code, datasets, model files, or third-party assets before distributing the service.

9. Suspension and changes

Access may be restricted where necessary to protect users, records, or service integrity. These terms must be updated if the service's capabilities, providers, payment arrangements, or intended use materially change. The operator must publish the effective date and a contact for questions.

10. Contact

Project operator and contact: to be published before launch. These draft terms are not a substitute for legal advice and should not be treated as a final statement of enforceable rights or obligations until reviewed for the actual deployment.
