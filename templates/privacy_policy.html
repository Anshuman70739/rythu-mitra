Rythu Mitra / Data and privacy

Privacy policy

This page explains how the current Rythu Mitra prototype is designed to handle information. It must be reviewed against the final hosting, retention, and support arrangements before public launch.

Last updated: 15 October 2026. Version: pre-launch draft.

Before public launch: the project owner must add a monitored privacy contact, confirm retention and deletion procedures, and review this policy against the deployed configuration and applicable law. This page is not a certification of compliance.

1. Who operates this service

Rythu Mitra is an academic farmer decision-support project. The operating institution or legal entity, postal address, and privacy contact have not been supplied in this build. They must be published here before the service is offered publicly. Do not submit sensitive personal information to a deployment that has not identified its operator.

2. Information the application may handle

  • Account details: name, phone number, account role, preferred language, and profile fields such as district, mandal, village, land area, crop, soil type, irrigation, and farming experience when entered.
  • Farm records: plots, crop plans and tasks, diary notes, soil observations, inventory, expenses, income, seed records, service requests, scheme follow-up records, and other information entered into enabled modules.
  • Images and evidence: crop-health images, seed packet images, receipts, soil reports, and other supported evidence uploaded by a signed-in user. Supported images are decoded and re-encoded by the backend before private storage.
  • Operational events: the application records certain security and audit events, such as login outcomes and changes to records. Server logs may also contain operational diagnostic information.
  • Device-local information: the browser stores the chosen language and may store offline drafts in local storage. Drafts can contain farm information. Anyone with access to the same unlocked browser profile may be able to inspect that local data.

3. Why information is used

Information is used to create and maintain the account, show the user's records, process requested calculations, run configured crop or disease models, support authorized sharing, synchronize eligible offline drafts, protect the service, and troubleshoot errors. Do not enter exact public location details or information about another person unless the feature genuinely requires it and you have permission.

4. Models and external providers

When configured, weather queries may be sent to a weather provider, and mandi price queries may be sent to the Government of India's data.gov.in/AGMARKNET service. Those providers receive the request information needed to answer the query and may process technical connection metadata under their own terms. A crop or disease model may process the submitted input or image on the application server. Model results are estimates and may be wrong; they are not a confirmed diagnosis or official agricultural prescription.

This build does not establish that every external integration is configured. The interface should mark unavailable, cached, curated, and model-generated information clearly.

5. Sharing and access

Private records and images should be returned only after the backend verifies the user's session, role, and ownership rights. Some workflows intentionally share limited fields with an invited helper or expose moderated public listings. Administrators may access information required for defined moderation and operational duties. Do not assume that a record is public or private solely because of its appearance; use only the sharing controls documented in the application.

Hosting, database, backup, and external service providers may process information to operate the deployed service. The final operator must identify relevant providers and contractual arrangements before launch.

6. Cookies and local storage

The application uses a server session cookie for authentication and session security. The frontend stores the selected language and eligible offline drafts in browser local storage. Clearing browser storage can remove local drafts that have not been synchronized. Logging out or clearing local storage should not be treated as deletion of server-side account records.

7. Retention, deletion and your choices

Records and uploaded evidence can remain on the server until the operator deletes them under a defined retention process. This code version does not provide a complete account-deletion or full account-data-export workflow in the user interface. Before public launch, the operator must define retention periods, secure deletion, backup expiration, access requests, correction requests, and account closure procedures, and publish a monitored contact for those requests.

8. Security and limitations

The code includes safeguards such as password hashing, session controls, CSRF checks, role and ownership checks, and upload validation. No website or storage system can be guaranteed perfectly secure. The actual protection depends on correct configuration, updates, access control, backups, and hosting practices.

9. Children and other people's information

This service is designed for farming and related adult users. Do not create an account for a child or submit another person's personal information without a lawful basis and permission where required.

10. Policy changes and contact

This policy must be updated if data collection, integrations, hosting, sharing, or retention changes. Privacy contact: to be published by the project operator before launch. Do not launch the public service until this contact and the operator identity are provided.
