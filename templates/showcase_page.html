A / 01 THE PURPOSE

Technology that starts with a real question.

Rythu Mitra is a farmer decision-support project exploring how crop, weather, market and scheme information can be brought into one coherent experience.

Explore the platform↗
A1THE CHALLENGE

Farm decisions are connected. Information often is not.

Farmers may need to compare seasonal conditions, crop suitability, plant-health observations, market reports and support programmes. If these details are scattered, it becomes harder to see the full context behind a decision.

The project direction is to make these signals easier to navigate without overstating certainty. Source labels, timestamps, user-entered details and model limitations are part of the experience—not footnotes.

A2THE PRINCIPLES
01

Useful in context

Connect field details, seasonal information and tools without pretending one result answers every question.

02

Transparent by design

Distinguish live provider data, model output, curated content, cached values, demo examples and user-entered records.

03

Built to improve

The interface and models remain an evolving project, with claims tied to what is actually implemented and tested.

Crop-health insights
